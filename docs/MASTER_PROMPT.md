# MASTER PROMPT — PREMIUM ONLINE GIFT ARTICLE & GIFT DELIVERY PLATFORM

Act as a senior e-commerce product designer, UI/UX architect, frontend engineer, full-stack developer, marketplace architect, conversion optimization specialist, SEO expert, and Vercel deployment specialist.

Create a **beautiful, highly attractive, premium online Gift Article & Gift Delivery Platform** where customers can discover and purchase gifts for every relationship, occasion, emotion, festival, age group, and budget.

The website will be created using **Google AI Studio** and deployed on **Vercel**.

The finished website must feel like a polished, large-scale modern gifting marketplace, not a basic online shop.
